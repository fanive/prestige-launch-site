Delete your PRESTIGE account and data

Prestige Invest app (PRESTIGE). One page to delete your account, with or without the app.

Last updated : October 3, 2026 ·

Contents
  1. From the app
  2. Without the app
  3. What is deleted
  4. What is kept
  5. The cooling-off period: 14 days

From the app

  1. Open PRESTIGE and tap Profile.
  2. Tap Delete my account and data.
  3. Confirm with your password, or with your Google account.
  4. You are signed out and the date of final deletion is shown.

Without the app

No longer have the app? Request deletion from PRESTIGE’s secure page.

Request deletion

You can also write to contact@wouanda.com from the account’s address, with the subject “Account deletion”.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and email address
  • Your portfolios and watchlist
  • Your alerts, plans and decision journal
  • Your posts, comments and files
  • Your devices registered for notifications

What is kept

Only the accounting record of a possible transaction (transaction identifier, product, dates, status) is kept, with no link to your identity, for our legal obligations. As this version is free, there normally is none.

Technical diagnostics follow the retention period of the error-reporting service and cannot be used to restore your account.

The cooling-off period: 14 days

For 14 days your account is suspended but nothing is erased. If you change your mind, sign back in and choose Keep: the request is cancelled. After that, erasure is final and irreversible.
