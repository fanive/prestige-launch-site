You can lose money

Investing exposes you to a partial or total loss of capital. Past performance does not predict future results.

The risks to know

  • Market risk: the value of an investment rises and falls with markets, the economy and interest rates.
  • Liquidity risk: some assets are hard to sell, sometimes at an unfavourable price.
  • Currency risk: an asset in a foreign currency also moves with the exchange rate.
  • Concentration risk: betting everything on one sector, security or region amplifies possible losses.

What PRESTIGE is

A tool to understand and organise your thinking. PRESTIGE is not a regulated investment adviser, makes no buy or sell recommendation and does not take your personal situation into account.

Data may be delayed or incomplete; each figure shows its date. A reading or footprint describes a company from published figures: it is not a forecast.

Your responsibility

You remain solely responsible for your decisions. Before an important decision, consult a qualified professional who knows your situation.
