Data and dates

Analyses use available prices and financial figures. Check their date and displayed coverage before interpreting them. A missing value is not replaced with zero.

Charts and signals

Technical signals describe observed movements within a chart timeframe. An early signal can change before the candle closes. It does not guarantee future results.

AI explanations and credits

AI explanations are requested explicitly. The server supplies the credit balance, usage and reset date. News appears in its original language without automatic AI translation.

A generated explanation may be wrong. Compare it with published figures and documents. Charts and news do not require AI credits.

Support for your thinking

The footprint, comparisons and portfolio X-ray organise available data. They are neither forecasts nor personalised buy or sell recommendations.
