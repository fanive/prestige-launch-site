What PRESTIGE will never do.

Tell you what to buy
It explains, you decide. It is not an investment adviser.
Show a figure without a date
Every price and result carries its date and source. A missing figure is said to be missing.
Keep you hooked for nothing
No streak to keep, no alert to bring you back. A notification means one of your prices was reached.
