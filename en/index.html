Understand

A company read in five seconds.

Published figures, translated into plain words. Always with their source and date.

1

The price, the chart, the signals.

A security fits one screen: its price with its date, a year of chart, and the technical signals spotted, marked on the curve.

2

The PRESTIGE footprint.

Five axes — growth, profitability, momentum, solidity, price — computed from published figures. It describes the company, never recommends.

3

What is going well, what to watch.

Each axis becomes one sentence: “out of 100 of sales, 66 remain after costs”. No jargon, no ratio to decode.

4

Ten chapters to understand it all.

What am I buying? Where do its sales come from? Why could it grow? The guided analysis answers question after question, at your pace.

Decide

Your plan, written calmly.

Before buying, or if you already own the security, answer a few questions. PRESTIGE watches the prices you pick and reminds you of your own plan the day one is reached.

  • Two paths. “I own it” or “I want to buy it”: the next questions adapt to your answer.
  • Quick marks. +10 %, +20 %, +30 % or the 52-week high: pick one, or type your own price.
  • A reminder at the right time. What you decided calmly, you read again when the market gets noisy.

The best time to decide is when the market is calm.

Markets

The whole market at a glance.

The S&P 500 map shows every sector and its largest companies. Area tells their weight, colour their move over the day, week, month or year.

  • Tap a sector : its curve, what moved it, its industries.
  • Open an industry : the main holdings of its ETF, one by one.
  • Change the period : the map, the curve and the figures follow.

FAQ

Common questions

Is PRESTIGE really free?

Yes. All features are free, with no subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising.

Can I buy or sell shares in the app?

No. PRESTIGE is not a broker. It helps you understand and decide; you place your orders with your usual intermediary.

Does PRESTIGE tell me what to buy?

No. PRESTIGE explains and organises your thinking. It is neither a recommendation nor investment advice.

Where does the data come from?

From market sources and documents published by companies. Every figure shows its date; prices may be delayed.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Delete my account and data. Or from the deletion page. A 14-day cooling-off period applies, then everything is erased.

Are notifications intrusive?

No: you choose the alert types, the securities and their frequency. Without a useful change, PRESTIGE does not write to you.

On which phones?

PRESTIGE is built for Android. Its Google Play release is being prepared.
